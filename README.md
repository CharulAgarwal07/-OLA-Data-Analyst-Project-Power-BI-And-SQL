# 🚖 OLA Ride Booking Operations Analysis

**SQL | Power BI | Data Analytics | Business Intelligence**

A comprehensive data analytics project focused on understanding ride-booking operations, customer behavior, cancellations, revenue trends, and driver performance using SQL and Power BI.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes OLA ride-booking data to uncover operational inefficiencies and business opportunities. Using SQL for data analysis and Power BI for visualization, the project provides actionable insights into ride demand, cancellation patterns, customer satisfaction, and revenue performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze ride booking trends and operational performance
* Identify key reasons for ride cancellations
* Evaluate driver and customer rating patterns
* Measure revenue contribution across payment methods
* Discover high-value customers and vehicle preferences
* Build an interactive dashboard for decision-making

## 📊 Key Insights

* Ride demand varies significantly across vehicle categories.
* UPI emerged as one of the most frequently used payment methods.
* Certain vehicle categories generate higher revenue despite lower booking volume.
* Driver-related cancellations contribute significantly to operational inefficiencies.
* Customer satisfaction strongly correlates with ride completion rates.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Reduce Cancellation Rates

Implement faster driver allocation and proactive customer communication.

### 2. Improve Driver Performance

Provide incentives and training programs for low-rated drivers.

### 3. Optimize Vehicle Allocation

Deploy high-demand vehicle types during peak hours.

### 4. Increase Customer Retention

Reward repeat customers through loyalty programs and personalized offers.

### 5. Revenue Growth Strategy

Promote digital payment methods and optimize pricing during peak demand periods.
